Privacy Policy — Post Library

Effective date: 18 July 2026 · Last updated: 29 July 2026

The short version: everything Post Library saves stays on your own computer. The developer operates no servers, collects no data, and never sees anything you save. The only time any data leaves your device is if you choose to run AI categorization, which sends post text directly from your browser to the AI provider you picked, using your own API key.

What Post Library does

Post Library saves posts from your own logged-in account's Saved list into a private, searchable library on your device, so you can browse and organize them offline. It works only on content your own account can already see.

Data the developer collects

None. Post Library has no analytics, no tracking, no telemetry, no ads and no developer-operated servers. Nothing you save, type or configure is ever transmitted to the developer.

Where your data lives

  • Your library (post text, replies, categories, thumbnails, imported videos) is stored in the browser's local extension storage (IndexedDB / chrome.storage) on your device.
  • Media files and the offline backup page (backup.html) are written to a folder you choose inside your Downloads folder.
  • Settings and API keys are stored in local extension storage on your device only.

Optional AI categorization

Off by default. If you choose to run it, the text of your selected posts (never media files) is sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you selected — Google Gemini or Agnes AI — authenticated with your own API key. The developer is not part of that connection and cannot see it. The provider's own privacy policy governs its handling of that text. Your API key is stored only on your device and is only ever sent to that provider itself.

OCR (reading text inside images)

Optional, off by default, and runs entirely on your device using a bundled engine. Images are never uploaded anywhere.

Data sharing and selling

Post Library shares no data with anyone and sells nothing. There is nothing to share — the developer holds no user data.

Deleting your data

You are in full control.

  • Deleting one post (or a batch, via Declutter) asks whether to delete that post's downloaded photos and videos as well. The box is unticked by default: leave it alone and the files stay on your disk untouched.
  • Clear library removes every record, and its final step offers the same choice for all of the downloaded files at once.
  • When you do ask for files to be deleted, the extension only ever removes files it downloaded itself, sitting inside the post's own folder within the library folder your Save to Downloads/ setting points at. It cannot reach anything else in your Downloads folder, and it tells you afterwards exactly how many files were deleted and how many it could not find.
  • You can of course still delete the media folder yourself like any normal files, and uninstalling the extension removes its local storage. Nothing persists anywhere else.

Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

Post Library's use of information received from browser APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
